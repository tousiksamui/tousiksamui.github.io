Tousik Samui

Postdoctoral Fellow, The Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai, India

I am a theoretical particle physicist. I study how new physics, and the Standard Model itself, show up in the data of high-energy colliders: the LHC today, and future machines such as the FCC-ee and a muon collider. Much of my work is on jets and jet substructure, machine-learning methods for collider data, and the software tools these studies need.

I did my PhD at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai. Since then I have been a postdoctoral fellow at the Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI), Prayagraj, at IISER Kolkata and, since 2024, at IMSc Chennai. I wrote DynamicR, the FastJet Contrib package for the Dynamic Radius jet algorithm.
